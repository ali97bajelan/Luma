# T08 People implementation plan

> Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan inline.

**Goal:** Offline people list/create/edit/detail and multiple-person references during supported capture/edit, with chronological linked history.

**Architecture:** Reuse v1 person/event_person tables. PersonRepository owns catalog CRUD and transactional graph history. LifeEventRecord carries people; capture inputs gain optional person-ID snapshots (null preserves prior links, empty explicitly clears). Existing saved editors retain selections and share a lifecycle-aware picker.

**Tech Stack:** Existing Kotlin, Room, Compose, Flow and JUnit; no new dependencies.

**Spec:** TASKS.md T08 and ARCHITECTURE.md.

## Constraints and decisions
- Domain remains free of Android/Room. No schema migration, permissions, network or contacts import.
- Person names required, <=200 characters; optional notes <=10,000. Preserve existing photo URI; no photo workflow in T08.
- Stable UUID identities allow distinct people with the same name. Identical create retries preserve identity.
- Person deletion is idempotent and cascades links only, invalidating derived summaries transactionally.
- Select up to 100 people per saved draft. Missing/deleted selections remain visible and removable; failed entry writes retain draft.
- Mood, journal, direct-memory and promoted-memory editors pass explicit selected IDs. Source links belong to the original event.
- Picker offers person creation and retains parent editor draft on return. Full people list has its own primary tab.

## Review focus
- Person create/edit/delete errors and ambiguous committed results cannot lose drafts or recreate deleted rows.
- Missing references must roll back envelope/content/links/cache as one transaction.
- Default/null people inputs preserve existing links; empty selections clear only people links.
- Deleting a person must retain all life events, typed details, other links and photo URI on unrelated catalog edits.
- Linked history has stable chronology, reflects people/event edits/deletes and supports opening original entries.
